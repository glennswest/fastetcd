## 3. Open issues: prior art

- **Vendored openraft patch (#97, upstream [databendlabs/openraft#2163](https://github.com/databendlabs/openraft/pull/2163))**: the maintainer replied on 2026-10-15. They will not backport to 0.9 (0.10 "coming soon"; main already commits before the leader's local flush once a durable quorum exists), and found three defects in the patch:
  1. `wait_if_leader_flush_stalled()` keeps waiting after a flush error while a later flush is pending, so RaftCore cannot see the fatal error or shutdown.
  2. `LeaderFlush::times` is pruned only by `stalled()`, called from `Command::Replicate`; a single-node cluster never issues that, so the queue grows by one entry per append. This is in our vendored copy (`vendor/openraft/src/core/raft_core.rs`, `stalled`). Filed.
  3. `watch::Receiver::wait_for` needs tokio 1.28 while openraft allows 1.22 (irrelevant to us, locked at 1.52).
  openraft 0.10 is still alpha (alpha.37, Oct 10); no 0.9.x since 0.9.25 (Jul 28). So the vendor copy stays until 0.10 is stable.
- #57 (MoveLeader): openraft alpha.36 (Sep 29) added `reset_backoff_on_transfer_leader`, and alpha.25 fixed stale transfer-target elections (#1806): useful reference for our backport design (target election after catch-up, stale-target rejection).
